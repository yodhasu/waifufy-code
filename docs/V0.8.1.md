# Waifufy Code 0.8.1: her in 3D, closer to life

A patch for 0.8.0 from the first day with a 3D card: she turns the right way and from the waist up, breathes, can be zoomed and looked at from another angle, takes headpats, and her face fits what she says. The voice learns how to say her name, and a card swap no longer takes your preset library with it.

## 3D (`app/src/character/mmd.ts`, `mmd-engine.ts`, `interact.ts`)

- **Cursor tracking turned the wrong way.** A cursor to your right now turns her to your right.
- **She follows the cursor across the screen**, not just inside the window: outside it the desktop shell reports the cursor (`cursorPosition`), read about 8 times a second.
- **From the waist up.** Following the cursor, her emotion stances and their beats now carry down through her neck, chest and waist instead of turning only her head.
- **Breathing.** A breath every ~4.4 s: her chest rises and opens, her shoulders lift, and her neck keeps her head level.
- **Headpats.** A stroke on her head makes her lean into it, and the Live2D headpat and tap tiers apply (happy, then shy, then a pout for a long run; taps surprise, then annoy), with their hearts and marks.
- **Zoom and camera.** The wheel over her zooms, for Live2D and 3D (Settings → Appearance → Framing, now 0.6×–2.5×). Zooming a 3D model keeps her head in frame and closes on her face. On a 3D card, a right-drag turns the camera round her, a middle-drag (or Shift + right-drag) moves it up and down, and a right double-click puts it back. She keeps facing you and following the cursor wherever the camera is.
- **Beside Settings she stands taller** (0.93× instead of the Live2D 0.74×), with her head about level with the panels' top edge.
- **Her face fits her words.** While she talks, her expression's mouth shapes give way to the lip sync (a laughing mouth flapping read as laughing at everything). A greeting smiles instead of laughing, and an app event (a greeting, a success) no longer replaces the emotion of what she is saying. The persona now asks for a fitting tag on each sentence: a question gets [think] (or [surprised]), never [smile].

## Voice

- **Pronunciation** (Settings → Voice): words the voice says wrong, one per line (`Pavolia Reine = Pah-VOH-lee-ah RAY-neh`). Only the voice uses them; the text keeps the real spelling. Whole words, longest first.
- **"How she speaks …" follows the voice language.** For an English voice the notes go into her persona too, and the Japanese subtitle switch only shows for a Japanese voice.
- **Research writes both for you.** A Roleplay research now also returns how she speaks in your voice's language and the respellings for her name, series and catchphrases; Apply puts them into Settings → Voice.

## Waifu cards

- **The preset library is shared.** In 0.8.0 each card carried the whole library, so swapping cards swapped it (and looked like it had been wiped). Now every card sees every researched preset; a card keeps only the one she plays, so an export still carries it.
- **No edit is lost to a swap or an older version.** A swap writes the settings back to the card it leaves, and at startup the active card is brought up to date with `settings.json` (which an older version of the app may have edited without knowing about cards).
