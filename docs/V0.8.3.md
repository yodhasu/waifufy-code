# Waifufy Code 0.8.3: a phone that stays connected, and a low-spec mode

0.8.2 got Reine onto the phone in 3D. Using it showed three problems: a phone that lost its connection often couldn't get back without a trip to the computer, she was framed wrong on the phone, and 3D was slow there. 0.8.3 fixes the first two, makes the phone's 3D lighter, and adds a low-spec mode that drops the character entirely.

## A phone that stays connected

Four things kept a phone out after a drop:

- **It only retried the address it was using.** When the network changed (leaving home Wi-Fi), it kept knocking on the dead LAN address and never tried the Tailscale one that would have worked. Now it tries every address it knows **at once**, again and again (a pause of up to 5 s between rounds), and uses whichever answers.
- **At launch, an unreachable computer threw the pairing away** and sent the phone to the QR scanner. Now it shows *Waiting for your computer…* and keeps trying on its own; it connects as soon as the computer answers. *Pair again* is there, but nothing forces it.
- **It never learned the computer's new addresses.** The computer now tells a phone its current addresses each time it connects (`hello.addresses`), and the phone keeps them with its pairing.
- **Closing the desktop window quit the app**, and the phone's link with it. Now, while phone access is on, the window's close button only hides it: Waifufy Code stays in the tray (click it to come back; *Quit* there, or Exit on the title screen, ends it). It is single-instance too: launching it again brings the window back instead of starting a second copy that would fight for the phone port.

**Each phone is paired for good.** The QR code now only gets a phone in: a phone that shows it gets a token of its own back (`paired`), keeps it, and reconnects with it from then on. So *New pairing code* only changes what new phones scan; it no longer unpairs anyone. Settings → Phone lists the paired phones (model, connected or last seen, when paired), each with *Remove*, which disconnects it and refuses its token. Phones paired before 0.8.3 are moved over on their next connection, on their own.

The phone asks to pair again only when the computer says, plainly, that it doesn't take its token any more (`/paired` answers 401). A slow or missing network is never taken for that: it just keeps trying.

**An emergency key.** The pairing link stays valid until a new code is made, so it is the way back in without the computer's screen:
- The phone keeps the link it was paired with. If it ever loses its own token, it pairs again with that link by itself.
- Keep a copy of the link too (Settings → Phone → Copy pairing link, into your notes or a password manager). After a reinstall, or with the app's data cleared, pasting it on the phone's pairing screen brings it back.
- Removing a phone also makes a new code, so the link saved on a removed (say, lost) phone can't bring it back. Copy the new link then.

Only a phone that asks for one (0.8.3 on) gets a token of its own: an older app keeps using the code as before, so it can't fill the list with copies of itself.

## Reine on the phone

- **Framed from the waist up in the phone's chat**, her head centered, filling the space between the chips and the dialogue box (the dialogue box covered her legs anyway). The full-body shot stays everywhere else.
- **Zoom closes in on her head**: its center stays put on screen, and the top of her head always stays in frame.
- **Lighter on the phone**: no MMD outline pass (it drew her twice), 1× pixel density, physics stepped 30 times a second instead of 60, and face morphs re-uploaded only when they change (also on the desktop).

## Low-spec mode (Settings → Phone, on the phone)

For a phone where even that is too slow: no character at all (no Live2D, no 3D, nothing loaded for them), and the chat becomes a chat room: your messages and hers as bubbles, her steps as one-line notes, the session's history when you join. Her voice still reads each line (the dialogue keeps running unseen, moving on by itself), and choices, prompts, the composer and the panels work as before. The visual-novel tutorial and the AUTO and SKIP tiles are left out. It is the phone's own setting, kept on the phone.

## Tests

- `tests/unit/remote.test.mjs`: a phone pairing with the code gets a device token and is told the computer's addresses; a new code keeps paired phones (the old code is refused, the device token works, the list never carries tokens); removing a device closes it as unpaired and refuses its token.
- `tests/e2e/scenarios/26-phone.mjs`: a phone that can't reach its computer waits and keeps its pairing, then connects on its own once it can; a phone keeps its pairing through a new code and is dropped to the pairing screen when removed; low-spec mode loads no character and shows the session as a chat room.

Not tested automatically: the tray and the single-instance guard (they need the real desktop shell), and the phone's speed (measured by eye on the device).
