# Waifufy Code 0.8.4: pose her yourself

0.8.1 gave a 3D card its built-in emotions: a face, a small stance, a beat. 0.8.4 lets you make your own. Every emotion can have a motion of yours instead: the pose she holds, and keys on the way into it. Make it in the pose studio by dragging her into place, or open it in Blender for a real animation. Either way it is saved on her waifu card.

## The pose studio (Settings → Appearance → Motions → Pose her)

She stands large at the left, with a dot on each joint you can move; the panel at the right holds the rest.

- **Drag a hand** and her arm follows it to where you put it (two-bone IK, the elbow keeping the side it bends to). **Drag an elbow** to swing the arm round with the hand staying put.
- **Her body keeps her hands out**: a hand pushed into her chest, or her face, stops at its surface, and an arm that would go through her swings round it. Her shape comes from the model's own rigid bodies, so this works for any MMD model, not just Reine.
- **Drag her head, neck, chest, waist or a shoulder** to turn it: across turns, down bends (Shift tilts); a shoulder shrugs. Every joint stays within how far it really turns.
- **Click a dot** for rotation rings that turn that joint exactly, or pick any joint (fingers and legs included) and use its three sliders.
- **Fingers**: a grid for each hand, a button per segment from base to tip; a click picks it, with its rings and sliders.
- **Hand shapes** for each hand: open, relaxed, fist, point, peace. **Mirror** one side onto the other. **Undo / Redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y).
- **Keys**: the pose she holds, plus up to 30 keys on the way into it, each at its time; ▶ plays it.
- **Back to built-in** gives the emotion its built-in motion again. **Save photo** saves her as she stands, as a JPEG.
- **Every change saves itself** on her card for the emotion picked; "✓ Saved" shows as it does, and the emotion reads *Her own, on her card*.
- **Camera**: ◀ ▶ turn it round her, *Front*, *Side* and *Back* put it there; in the studio it goes all the way round (elsewhere it keeps its usual reach, and leaving the studio brings it back). Right-drag turns it too, the wheel zooms, a right double-click puts it back. Esc puts the rings away, then leaves.

While you pose her she holds still (no breathing or looking about), and headpats and taps wait until you leave.

## Open in Blender

For a motion the studio can't make, the studio's *Open in Blender* opens her model in your Blender with the emotion's motion on it. Edit it there and save (Ctrl+S): each save comes straight back to the app and onto her card. Nothing to export, no file to pick.

- The app finds Blender on its own (the program that opens .blend files, then the usual install folders); if it can't, it asks for blender.exe once and remembers it.
- It needs Blender's **MMD Tools** extension. The app turns it on if it is installed but off; if it isn't installed, the app says how to get it (Edit → Preferences → Get Extensions in Blender).
- Blender keys what you move (auto-keying is on in the file it opens), so a pose changed without a keyframe isn't lost.
- What comes back is the joints the app moves: her upper body, arms, hands and legs. Face morphs, センター and IK targets stay in Blender.
- Blender opens on the desktop only; a phone shows the motions but doesn't start Blender.

A motion travels with the card: exported and imported cards keep their motions (cleaned on the way in: real emotions only, turns within ±180°, at most 30 keys within 10 s).

## On the phone

Measured on a mid-range phone (OPPO CPH2711: Snapdragon "bengal", Adreno 610, 90 Hz screen) over USB.

- **She runs at 60 fps** (she was capped at 30), for less CPU than 30 fps cost before: about 310% of a core in the chat against 365% before. What changed:
  - The app asks Android for a **60 Hz screen** while it is open. Every frame the WebView draws costs about the same CPU whatever is on it, so a 90 Hz screen spent a third more just redrawing.
  - Her canvas has **no MSAA and no kept drawing buffer** on a phone. Babylon's constructor argument switched MSAA back on whatever the options said.
  - In the chat's waist-up shot, the **physics chains hanging from her waist rest** (her skirt: 80 of Reine's 155 bodies), since the dialogue box covers them. Physics went from 5.4 to about 2 ms a frame. Any MMD model: it is the chains hanging from 下半身.
  - **When a phone can't keep 60** (a slow one, or one hot and throttled) she drops to 30 fps for 20 s, and the page's petals and ▼ rest, so the phone cools; then 60 is tried again.
  - **With her hidden** (Settings) the petals rest: the page idles at about 27% CPU instead of 250%.
  - The frame cap no longer drops a frame each time a phone's frame clock comes a little early.
- **Pinch to zoom her**, two fingers on her (the second may land anywhere).
- **Headpats and taps work on the phone.** They never did: her canvas only took the pointer while a mouse hovered over her, and a finger doesn't hover.
- **Sideways (Theater) fills the screen**: the status and navigation bars hide (a swipe from the edge shows them), and the stage runs under the camera cutout instead of inside a black band.
- **The app can't be zoomed like a web page** any more (pinch or double-tap).
- **Settings sideways** uses the phone layout (it was the desktop's, at about 5 pt); the tab strip scrolls the open tab into view and fades at its edges so the other tabs show. She isn't drawn beside Settings on a phone.
- **The title sideways** fits all six rows (Exit was cut off) at a size a thumb can hit; **Save/Load sideways** no longer runs each slot's text under the next row, and its actions always show.
- The composer's placeholder stays on one line on a phone.
- **No pose studio on the phone**: its screen, the posing code and the rotation rings are left out of the phone build (the Motions card is hidden there). Her motions still come with the card.

## Fixes

- **Lip sync off means off**: with Settings → Lip sync off, her mouth no longer follows her voice either (it only stopped the typing flap before).
- **Her lips close between syllables (3D)**: an emotion whose mouth is open (a laugh, an "o", a pout) lets go of it while she talks, so lip sync starts from closed lips instead of the emotion's open mouth.
- **No more jitter (3D)**: her chest, hair and skirt shook even when she stood still. Her physics took fixed 1/60 s steps while she is drawn at another rate (every third frame of a 144 Hz screen, 48 a second), so most frames took one step and some two, and Babylon-mmd shows each step as it is: those frames jumped. Each frame is now cut into equal steps (at most 1/120 s), the same number every frame, by the time since she was last drawn. Measured on a 144 Hz screen: her chest's largest frame-to-frame jumps about a third of what they were.
- **No more jitter on the phone (3D)**: a phone took one physics step per frame of up to 1/30 s, so each time it ran a frame late the step doubled in length and her chest jumped. Its steps are now at most 1/60 s: a late frame takes two even steps, so the step stays the same length (and costs the same at 60 fps).

## Under the hood

- The pose studio's collision with her body read her rigid bodies after babylon-mmd had trimmed them off the model, so it was empty on a real model: hands could pass into her chest. They are read before the model is made now.
- On the desktop her 3D canvas could never take the pointer outside the studio (an inline style beat the stylesheet), so headpats, taps and the right-drag camera over her in the chat did nothing on a 3D card. Fixed with the phone's touches.
- The frame cap takes a frame when three quarters of her frame time has passed: on a 144 Hz desktop screen she now draws every second frame (72 fps; it was every third, 48).
- The rotation rings (Babylon's gizmo, its utility layer and picking) load the first time a joint is picked, not with every 3D card.

- Motions are kept as the card has always kept them: turns per joint, in degrees, over her relaxed pose. They now keep a tenth of a degree and a hundredth of a second.
- VMD is read and written by the app itself (`app/src/character/vmd.ts`; Shift-JIS bone names encoded with a table built from the browser's own decoder). The same file goes to Blender and comes back.
- Two Babylon modules the 3D view never needed before are loaded now (the picking ray and the default material): without them the rotation rings could be seen but never dragged. Babylon's pointer is also corrected for the stage's scale, which it doesn't know about.
